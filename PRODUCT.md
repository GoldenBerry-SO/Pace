# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Everyone at GoldenBerry who uses an AI coding agent (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, and others) and wants a shared, repeatable way to do work. Engineers running PR reviews. PMs writing specs. Ops folks on runbooks. Marketing on launch copy. Not just designers — the whole company.

They land on `pace.tools` from internal docs, Slack, or word of mouth. They already know what skills are; they want the ones that match how GoldenBerry works.

## Product Purpose

Pace gives the company a shared vocabulary with our AI tools. Each command is a repeatable process — codified once, used everywhere, by humans and agents alike. Success is measured two ways: (1) the company stops having "how do we do X here?" conversations because the answer is `/pace x`, and (2) AI output stops drifting from how we actually work, because the skill teaches it our way.

For frontend design specifically, pace defers to **impeccable**. Pace's installer offers impeccable as a paired install so design fluency comes from the source.

## Brand Personality

Practical, opinionated, low-ceremony. Pace speaks like a senior teammate who's seen the company's patterns enough times to know what works: confident in the way we do things, light on hype, allergic to "best practices" prose. The tone is **direct** (no "consider doing"), **specific** (commands name what they do), and **shared** (we is the operative pronoun — this is how *we* work).

Three-word personality: **practical, opinionated, shared**.

## Anti-references

The site and brand should not look or read like:

- **Generic productivity SaaS.** Stock photos, gradient hero sections, "boost your team's velocity" copy, cards-on-cards layouts.
- **Hedging language.** "Might want to consider", "could potentially help". Pace picks a way and commits.
- **Education-heavy framing.** Users land on pace because they already know what skills are and want ours. Don't teach the category; ship the commands.
- **Over-decoration.** Every visual element earns its place.

## Design Principles

1. **Match impeccable in craft, not in scope.** Pace borrows the install tech and the editorial bar. The content is its own thing.
2. **Show the router.** The homepage's job is to make the command list legible. Not a hero, not a fold full of feature cards — the list.
3. **Pair, don't duplicate.** Pace links to impeccable for design work. The handoff is part of the product.
4. **Confidence without volume.** Direct and opinionated, but not loud. Editorial restraint over marketing energy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA on every page. Tokens verified for contrast, focus states visible, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, semantic HTML first.
